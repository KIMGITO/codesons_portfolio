# Keyword plan

Keywords are the phrases customers type into Google. Give **each page one main phrase**, use it naturally in the
title, first paragraph and a heading, and never target the same phrase with two pages.

How to find ideas (free): type your service into Google and read the **suggestions**, **People also ask** and
**Related searches**; check **Google Search Console > Performance** for phrases you already show up for;
**Google Trends** (trends.google.com) to compare phrases in Kenya.

## Writing rules of thumb
* Title: main phrase near the start, under about 60 characters, ending with `| Codensons`.
* Description: under about 155 characters, say what the visitor gets and invite them to act ("Get a free quote").
* One H1 per page. Use H2s for sections.
* First 100 words: mention the phrase and your city naturally.
* Add 2 to 3 internal links to other pages, and descriptive link text ("website design pricing", not "click here").
